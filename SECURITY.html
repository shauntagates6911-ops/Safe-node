# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability
If you find a security issue, please email us at: shaunta.gates6911@gmail.com

## Supported Versions
We currently support all live pages on Safe-node.

## Responsible Disclosure
Please do not publicly share vulnerabilities before we have time to fix them.

## Scope
This policy applies to all pages under safe.github.io and safe-node.netlify.app.

## Out of Scope
Automated spam, broken links, and non-security bugs.
